The vulnerability scanner built for community organisations.

Diana checks a small nonprofit's website and email setup for the weaknesses that lead to ransomware and fraud, then explains each one in plain language: what it means, why it matters, and the exact fix, with ready-to-send Dutch emails for the organisation's webmaster. Under the hood, Diana runs the proven CERT-PL Artemis engine and adds the layer small organisations actually need: clear, NGO-focused guidance instead of a technical report nobody can read.

How Diana works. Diana runs the proven CERT-PL Artemis engine to scan, then focuses on the vulnerability types most relevant to small nonprofits (email spoofing, exposed files, outdated CMS, known CVEs, risky open services) and explains each in plain language. Any finding from the engine that Diana doesn't yet have a plain-language explanation for is listed separately at the bottom, honestly, rather than dropped, so nothing is hidden.
